# Voyage Checklist — Before, During, After

*A one-page trip card. Print it or keep it open in a second window. Check things off in your
head; this list doesn't track you, it just reminds you. Part of the hand-maintained, gentle-ethos
manual — no automation, no account data.*

Last hand-checked: **2026-10-31**

## Before you board

- [ ] Fisher gear equipped and repaired (nothing fancy; mid-level gear sails fine)
- [ ] Duty Finder open, Ocean Fishing duty located
- [ ] Departure window checked against the table in `01-voyage-windows.md`
- [ ] Bait tray glance: know which bait is your light, medium, and heavy picks
- [ ] One tiny goal written down (see `06-patience-tips.md`)
- [ ] Ten minutes of actual free time confirmed, so you won't have to jump ship mid-track

## On the boat

- [ ] Wave to your fellow fishers; they're the scenery that talks
- [ ] Read the fish-finder at the start of *every* stretch
- [ ] Follow `04-bait-rotation-cards.md` Card 1 until something interesting happens
- [ ] Call out any boil you see — even if you're mid-reel
- [ ] If Spectral waters open: best bait, quick recasts, enjoy rather than optimize
- [ ] If the sea goes slow: scenery time, no guilt

## After the trip

- [ ] Note your score (or bronze/silver/gold feeling) in your own log
- [ ] Update the boil log in `02-hourly-boils.md` if something surprising happened
- [ ] Update the weather row in `03-weather-and-expectations.md` if your sky was unusual
- [ ] Move the verify date on any table you touched
- [ ] Achievement Log glance: any bucket from `05-achievement-hunts.md` edge closer?
- [ ] Decide whether the next voyage is "go again" or "see you tomorrow" — both answers are
      correct
