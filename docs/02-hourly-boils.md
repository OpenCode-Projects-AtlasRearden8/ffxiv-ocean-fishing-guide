# 2. Hourly Boils — Reading the Ocean's Splash

*A "boil" is just what it sounds like: a patch of water suddenly boiling with fish feeding at the
surface. On The Endeavor, boils are there to be noticed, fished, and shared — never to be
grinded for.*

**Hand-maintained, not automated.** No sniffing, no scraping, no account data. Every figure on

## What a boil is, in plain words

- Mid-voyage, fish sometimes churn the surface in one spot. You'll see splash rings — that's the
  boil.
- Fish hooked *inside* a boil usually pull bigger points than the same patience-fishing elsewhere
  on deck.
- Boils don't last long. If you're in the middle of reeling something else in, finish politely,
  then cast toward the splash. Nobody is scoring you on reaction speed; there's always another
  voyage.
- Chat tip: call out boils to the boat ("boil starboard!") whether or not you hook it. Pointing
  people at boils is one of the small kindnesses that keeps ocean fishing friendly.

## Boils and Spectral waters

When a boil coincides with **Spectral waters** (the glittery, rare-fish window), the boat's chat
usually lights up with joy. That's your cue to:

1. Use your best bait pairing (see the cheat cards in [`04-bait-rotation-cards.md`](04-bait-rotation-cards.md)).
2. Keep your line in and your mood kind — Spectral windows end, and the sea settles again.
3. Screenshot it. You'll want the memory even more than the fish.

## Patience reminder

You will miss boils. You will mis-cast into them. You will hook a boot. All of this is normal and
the scoreboard still likes you. The real prize of ocean fishing is thirty quiet minutes on
fictional water with other people pointing at fish. Slow seas stay fun exactly because they're
slow.
