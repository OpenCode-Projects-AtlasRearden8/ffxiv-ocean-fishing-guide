# 5. Achievement Hunts — For Relaxed Collectors

*Achievement-hunting on The Endeavor can be ghoulish or gentle. This page is the gentle
version: goals that feel like souvenirs, not invoices.*

**Hand-maintained, not automated.** Achievement names evolve with patches and localizations, so
this page points at *categories* in your in-game achievement log and describes the hunt, rather
than hard-coding strings that might drift. Last hand-checked: **2026-10-31**

## How to find the achievements

Open your **Achievement Log** and look in the fishing section for the ocean-fishing entries.
They generally fall into four friendly buckets:

1. **Points-per-voyage tiers** — earn a target score in one trip (bronze/silver/gold style
   ladders). Each tier is "the same voyage, plus a bit more luck and bait sense."
2. **Lifetime totals** — cumulative points across many voyages. This is the meta-achievement;
   it fills in itself while you're being a normal, happy boat person.
3. **Catch-list goals** — land specific named fish, often the Spectral-window show-offs or
   route specialties. These are the collector's darlings.
4. **Special-condition catches** — fish that only appear under certain skies or at certain
   voyage moments. Perfect matches for the boil log in `02-hourly-boils.md`.

## The relaxed collector's path

| Order | Hunt | Why it's gentle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Finish 3 voyages (bronze scores) | No skill ceiling; learn the deck at bronze pace |
| 2 | One silver-score voyage | Arrives naturally after a few calm rotations |
| 3 | Catch one Spectral-window fish | Requires luck, not perfection; windows appear on most evenings |
| 4 | Log a boil-specialty catch | Married to your personal boil log — hunting by memory |
| 5 | A points-per-voyage tier of your choice | Stop whenever it stops being fun; resume next window |
| 6 | Lifetime point milestone | Passive; it grows while you're doing anything else on this list |

## Title-and-glamour souvenirs

Some of the above award titles and, eventually, fishing-glamour trinkets. If a title makes you
smile, chase it; if the hunt stops being pleasant, the game is telling you the souvenir's price
went up this week. Souvenirs can wait. The boat doesn't mind.

## A note on "efficiency" content

There are community resources that compute exact bait math for record scores. This manual
deliberately does not replicate them — our promise here is plain tables, warm tone, and a build
you can test. Use those resources if you want record scores; use this manual if you want a nice
sail. Both are the right answer to different evenings.

## Tracking, by hand

- Keep your own tally in a notebook or in `voyage-checklist.md`'s "after the trip" section.
- Achievement progress is visible in the log itself; no plugin, no overlay, no account data
  extracted — that's the whole ethos of this project.
